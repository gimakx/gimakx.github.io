One price at every quantity

Badges are $1 per unit whether you order ten or five hundred. There is no volume break to chase here, so the count is a straightforward question of how many you will actually hand out. What does bite is the 10-unit minimum per design: it is applied per design, so two different crests are two runs of ten rather than one run of twenty.

Made to your artwork

The pair photographed here are 4″ square. That is the sample rather than a standard — badges are made to the artwork you send, so a crest that is not a tidy circle or square does not have to be redrawn to fit a stock outline, and the number of colors in it is yours to decide.

Scheduling

Badges sit on a 3–4 week cycle from the day an order is confirmed — the same window the GiPro and apparel lines work to. Ordered together, they finish together, rather than the patches following on later under freight of their own.

The whole ladder, one order

Every adult rank is made here — white, blue, purple, brown and black — along with kids’ ranks, in A0–A5 with youth lengths below that. An academy promoting across the full ladder can put every rank on a single purchase order rather than splitting one grading between two suppliers.

Where the price actually moves

Belts are the one line on this page where volume genuinely changes the rate. The table below runs from $5 down to $2 — a 60% fall between the smallest and largest tier. That is steep enough to be worth setting the quantity against a grading calendar rather than a single grading night, and it is the opposite of how the badges above are priced.

Minimums and timing

Ten units is the floor on any one design, and that is also how the tier gets counted. Allow 3–4 weeks once the order is confirmed — a belt run booked with a Gi order comes off the floor on the same timetable.

What a grading actually costs

Take an academy promoting 40 students and giving each of them a belt and an academy badge. At 40 units the belt sits in the middle tier at $3, so the belt line is $120. The badges are $1 each at any quantity, adding $40. The goods come to $160.

Forty is an awkward number to stop on. Ten more belts crosses into the top tier, where every belt in the run is priced at $2 — 50 belts for $100. The larger order is $20 cheaper than the smaller one and leaves ten belts in the cupboard for the next intake, bringing the same grading to $140.

The one thing that undoes this is splitting the run. Those 40 belts ordered as four separate ten-unit designs are four separate ten-unit runs, each priced at $5, which puts the belt line at $200 rather than $120.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you print academy branding on Gi bags?

Yes — we print your academy logo on Gi bags for a complete branded package. Gi bags are a popular add-on for academies ordering full uniform sets, keeping every element of your kit — from Gi to bag — consistently branded to your academy's identity.

Can I get fully custom badge and patch designs?

Yes. Every badge is embroidered, and Gimakx provides 100% customization over the design — any shape, any size, any color count, with your academy logo, colors and placement exactly as you specify.

Can belts be customized with academy branding?

Yes — custom embroidery is available on all belt ranks, with rank bar options included. This is particularly popular for grading events and new-student kit distributions where consistency across your academy matters.

Can we order a complete academy kit — Gis, apparel, and accessories — from one supplier?
