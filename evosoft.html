Your Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a custom designed rash guard for my academy?

Yes — Evosoft rash guards can be fully sublimated with your academy's logo, sponsors, and color themes. Because we use edge-to-edge sublimation printing, your design runs across the entire garment with no limits on color count or placement.

Are Evosoft rash guards suitable for both No-Gi and under-Gi training?

Yes. The Evosoft is made from 240gsm moisture-wicking, anti-bacterial, and stretchable fabric — ideal for No-Gi sessions as standalone competition gear, and designed to sit flat under the Gi without bunching or causing discomfort during rolls.

Will the rash guard design fade or peel after washing?

No. We use high-quality sublimation printing that permanently bonds the design into the fabric at a molecular level. The colors won't crack, peel, or fade — even after years of regular washing and hard training. This is the fundamental advantage of sublimation over screen printing or heat transfer.

What is the minimum order for Evosoft rash guards?

The minimum order is 10 units per design for the Evosoft custom series. This applies to each individual design — so if your academy wants two different color schemes, each design requires a minimum of 10 units.

Can the Evosoft be matched to our GiPro Gi order?

Absolutely. One of the primary reasons academies choose Gimakx is the ability to coordinate a complete kit from one manufacturer. Your Evosoft rash guard can be designed to match your GiPro Gi in colors, logo placement, and branding — so every athlete wears a unified academy identity from Gi to rash guard.

What is the exact fabric composition of the Evosoft?

90% polyester and 10% spandex, knitted to 240 GSM. The polyester is what the sublimation dye bonds to, which is why the print cannot crack or peel; the spandex is what gives the fabric its 4-way stretch and recovery. Both figures are fixed — every unit in your order is cut from the same specification.

Is the Evosoft fabric 4-way stretch?

Yes. The 240 GSM 90/10 polyester-spandex knit stretches along both the length and the width of the cloth and recovers to shape, which is what lets the garment hold compression through a round instead of bagging at the elbows and shoulders.

What seam construction do you use on the rash guard?

Flatlock stitch throughout. A flatlock seam joins the two panels edge to edge rather than folding them over each other, so there is no raised ridge sitting against the skin — which is what prevents chafing on long under-Gi sessions and stops the seam catching under a Gi jacket.

Are the rash guards tagless inside?

Yes. We supply the Evosoft tagless, with the size and care information printed directly inside the garment instead of on a sewn-in tag. There is nothing at the neck to scratch, curl, or be cut out by the athlete, and the printed label carries your branding on a private-label order.

What fabric are your No-Gi shorts made from?

The same fabric as the Evosoft rash guard — 240 GSM, 90% polyester and 10% spandex, with 4-way stretch, flatlock seams and tagless printed inner labels. Ordering shorts alongside rash guards means both pieces take the same sublimated artwork and behave the same way in the wash, because they are the same cloth.

How is the No-Gi shorts waistband constructed?

Our standard build is a hybrid closure waistband. We can also customize the closure to your specification — tell us what your athletes prefer and we build the waistband to match on your order.

Are the No-Gi shorts available unlined or compression-lined?

Both are available. Unlined is the single shell, worn over the athlete's own spats or compression shorts, and is the usual choice for grappling. Compression-lined has an inner compression short built into the waistband, so the garment is complete on its own. The price difference between the two is confirmed in your written quotation.

What do the model codes like ERGCS01 mean?

Because almost every order is a fresh custom design, we number designs rather than stock. The prefix is the garment — ERG for an Evosoft rash guard, ESH for Evosoft No-Gi shorts — CS marks it as a custom design, and the number is a running count across every design we have produced. A matched set shares one number, so ERGCS02 and ESHCS02 are the rash guard and shorts of the same design. Quote the code back to us on a repeat order and we produce from the same artwork and specification.
