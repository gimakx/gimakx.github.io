Frequently Asked Questions

Which BJJ Gi guide should I read first?

Read them in the order they appear on the guides page, first to last. The sequence is deliberate: it opens with the basics and moves to more advanced topics further down. Start with fabric weights, then work through the list. If you only need one specific answer, jump straight to the guide that covers it.

What GSM should a beginner's Gi be?

450 GSM for adult beginners and 380 GSM for kids. 450 GSM is heavy enough to take daily grip-fighting while you build technique, and it is the most ordered weight we make. 380 GSM is lighter and more breathable, which suits children and hot training rooms. The fabric weights guide compares all three weights in full.

How do I know which Gi size to order for my academy?

Size by your students' height and weight together, then order 3 to 4 units per size to cover the spread of a typical class. Because the minimum is 10 units per design and sizes can be mixed freely within that design, a first order of 10 usually lands as 3 or 4 units across two or three adjacent sizes. The size chart has the full height-by-weight tables.
